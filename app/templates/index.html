The ArtistFeel the soul

Zimbermanne is a music artist making Afro Soul Pop: smooth, melodic records built on groove, emotion and storytelling. His work speaks about love, rhythm and the connection between people, wrapped in a refined, cinematic style.

Each record is its own world. One glows with late-night city lights and romance; another steps into a snow-covered, mysterious chapter of diary-style confession; while the latest explores ambition, wealth, and industrial soul.

His StoryJust a listener, once

Zimbermanne did not begin as a star. He began exactly where you are now: a listener with headphones on, letting other people’s songs carry him through long days, quiet nights and the moments words failed him.

Music was always there. It held him when he was happy, and it sat beside him when he was not. Somewhere between one song and the next, he started to notice something: there were things he wished he could tell the world. Things about love and loss, about hard work and hope, about the struggles people carry in silence and the dreams they are afraid to say aloud.

If I have something to say, why keep it inside?

Today, every record is a letter to society, written in rhythm and soul. Some are tender, some are bold, and some carry the weight of stories people rarely hear. And behind each one is still that same listener, hoping that somewhere, someone presses play and feels a little less alone.
